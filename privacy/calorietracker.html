Privacy policy: CalorieTracker

CalorieTracker, published by ZugZug Labs for private testing. Effective 4 October 2026.

Summary

CalorieTracker keeps your food diary on your device. To estimate calories it sends the meal photos and food descriptions you choose to analyse to Anthropic's Claude API. Nothing else leaves your phone, and ZugZug Labs runs no server of its own.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (height, weight, age, activity level, goals), meal entries, photos, weight history and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data sent to a third party

  • Meal photos and descriptions you ask the app to analyse are sent to Anthropic (Anthropic Privacy Policy) to estimate calories and nutrients. Photos are resized before sending and are not stored by ZugZug Labs.
  • Approximate location (city or region, when you grant the permission) is sent with the analysis request so that local dishes and portion sizes are recognised. Precise coordinates are not sent.
  • Barcodes you scan are looked up in the public Open Food Facts database (Open Food Facts privacy).

Data we collect

ZugZug Labs collects nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph meals and scan barcodes.
  • Location (approximate): optional, to improve food recognition for local dishes.

Health information

The figures in the app are estimates for personal tracking and are not medical advice.
