Privacy policy: Sudoku

Sudoku: Daily Logic Puzzles, published by ZugZug Labs. Effective 4 October 2026.

Summary

Sudoku does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It works entirely offline.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, statistics, daily-challenge history, level and unlocked themes are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not connect to the internet.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions.

Purchases

If an optional in-app purchase is offered, the transaction is handled by Google Play. ZugZug Labs does not receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
